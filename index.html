WEBSITE

This website is a space to share what I build and for people to know me better. I started with a blank slate, no template. I really wanted to express my creativity through my personal website. Contrary to the previous version, this one is built with My stance on AI use has since flipped from my initial views in 2024-2025. I had viewed all AI use as a form of insincerity, hence why my first website was all hand-written code. This view was mostly influenced by the flood of AI art on social media which lacked creativity and felt incredibly exploitative. But I have come to recognize the many benefits. In the creation of this website, all ideas were original. I used AI not as an idea generator, but as a tool to translate my thoughts into code. . I plan to shape it over time as new projects and interests take form.

The earlier version is preserved separately. I designed this site to retain a more modern and sleek design. Orange was (maybe still is) my favorite color so I made it an ongoing theme throughout the site. I also made the site a lot more interactive to hold the viewers' attention.
