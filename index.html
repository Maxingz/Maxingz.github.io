ABOUT ME

I study electrical engineering at UBC, where I explore how ideas become systems you can build, test, and improve. I'm especially drawn to space exploration and the possibility of humanity one day traveling beyond our solar system.

Lab work and team projects have given me hands-on experience with electronics, rapid prototyping, and solving problems as they arise. I enjoy learning across disciplines and working with others to turn ambitious concepts into practical steps.
